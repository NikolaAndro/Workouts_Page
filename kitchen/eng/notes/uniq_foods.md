
# Bone Density & Bone Health

### Sardines (with bones)

An excellent source of calcium and vitamin D, which are key for maintaining strong bones.

### Kefir or Fermented Dairy/Non-Dairy Alternatives

Provide bioavailable calcium and beneficial probiotics that support bone health.

### Kale

A leafy green rich in calcium and vitamin K, which are essential for bone health.

### Collagen

Contributes to the integrity of cartilage, helping maintain joint function and reducing discomfort associated with aging or strenuous exercise.

# Blood Health

### Beetroot

High in dietary nitrates, beetroot can help improve blood flow and oxygen delivery.

### Spinach & Other Dark Leafy Greens

Packed with iron, folate, and vitamin K, they promote healthy blood and vascular function.

# Brain Function

### Walnuts

Their high levels of DHA (an omega‑3 fatty acid) can help boost brain performance.

### Dark Chocolate (at least 70%)

Its high levels of flavonoids have been linked to improved blood flow to the brain, enhancing cognitive performance and potentially supporting memory.

# Gut Microbiome

### Sauerkraut & Kimchi

These fermented cabbage products are rich in beneficial bacteria and fiber.

### Yogurt & Kefir

Look for versions with live and active cultures; they can help improve the balance of your gut flora.

### Miso & Tempeh

Fermented soy products that not only offer protein but also contain probiotics that aid in digestion.

### Fermented Beverages (like Kombucha)

They provide a variety of microbial strains that can help diversify your gut bacteria.

### Prebiotic Foods

Foods such as garlic, onions, bananas, asparagus, and whole grains feed the good bacteria in your gut and support overall digestive health.

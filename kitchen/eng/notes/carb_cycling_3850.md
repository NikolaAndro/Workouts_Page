### Why This Works

- ✅ **Aligns carbs to workout intensity** – More on heavy leg days, less on rest days.  
- ✅ **Creates a weekly caloric deficit (~500 kcal/day avg)** – Sustainable fat loss.  
- ✅ **Prevents metabolic adaptation** – Keeps metabolism from slowing down.  
- ✅ **Optimizes recovery & performance** – Fuel when needed, cut when not.  

---

### Meal Timing

- **High & Moderate Days:** Focus carbs pre/post-workout for performance & recovery.  
- **Low-Carb Days:** Prioritize healthy fats & protein to stabilize energy.  
- **Protein Stays High Daily (~240g):** For muscle preservation & growth.  

---

### Final Thoughts

This plan keeps you in a steady caloric deficit (~3346 kcal avg/day) for fat loss while allowing for muscle growth.  

- If you feel fatigued or performance drops, add **+100-200 kcal** from carbs on training days.  
- If fat loss stalls after 4-6 weeks, reduce low-carb day calories slightly (~2800 kcal).
